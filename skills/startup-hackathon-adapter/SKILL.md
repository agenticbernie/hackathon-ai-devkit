# startup-hackathon-adapter

## Goal
Map reusable hackathon capabilities without copying idea-first assumptions.

Hackathon workflows are usually idea-first; startup workflows should be problem-first.
Hackathon wow moments become startup proof of value, and demo scope becomes learning or
validation scope. Classify skills as reuse, adapt, or avoid and explain each dependency,
evaluation, and output change.
