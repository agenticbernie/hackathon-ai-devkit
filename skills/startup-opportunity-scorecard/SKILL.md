# startup-opportunity-scorecard

## Goal
Score and rank candidate pain points transparently so the next research step reduces
the most important uncertainty.

Use a 1–5 scale for severity, frequency, urgency, buyer access, and willingness to pay.
Additional dimensions may cover economic impact, workaround weakness, accessibility,
competition, switching friction, evidence confidence, complexity, and team advantage.

Every score must include a rationale, evidence reference, confidence, and status:
`validated`, `assumed`, or `hypothetical`. A high score is not proof of product-market
fit. Identify missing evidence that could change the ranking and actively recommend
`deep_dive`, `validate`, `collect_more_evidence`, `deprioritize`, or `reject`.
