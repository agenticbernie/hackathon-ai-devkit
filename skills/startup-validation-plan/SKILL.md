# startup-validation-plan

## Goal
Prioritize the riskiest and most important assumptions and assign each a test.

Every hypothesis must include a validation method, success threshold, falsification
threshold, evidence artifact, status, and next decision. Use evidence to decide whether
to continue, revise, or stop; do not treat a plan as validation.
