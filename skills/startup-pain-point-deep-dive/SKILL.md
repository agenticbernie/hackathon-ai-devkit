# startup-pain-point-deep-dive

## Goal
Determine whether one selected pain point is promising enough for structured validation.

## Rules
Map user, buyer, decision maker, triggers, workflow, alternatives, consequences,
switching barriers, and willingness-to-pay signals. Actively seek disconfirming evidence,
state conditions where the pain is unimportant, and do not automatically recommend a build.

Allowed verdicts: `promising`, `needs_validation`, `weak`, `invalidated`, `insufficient_evidence`.
